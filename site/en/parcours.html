THE ATHLETE BEHIND THE METHOD

VICTOR CASTILLO

I am 19. A L2 STAPS student, specialising in sports training, at Paris Cité, I am building an approach that combines scientific knowledge, university learning and hands-on field experience.

I started lifting alone, without a club, because an athlete must always work on their body: it is the machine that performs, in any sport.

A former competitive basketball player up to national level in the U15 and U18 youth categories, with county selections, I am now a middle-distance athlete. I have played more than 9 sports at different levels: basketball, athletics, swimming, rugby, football, judo, tennis, cycling, strength training and running. Three of them at regional level, plus county medals in judo.

I have been coaching since 2022, first people around me and my friends, then my family, and today athletes I met on social media. Sport has been at the centre of my life since I was 5-6 years old.

For me, physical activity is essential for health and well-being. It lets you look beyond work and studies, take care of yourself and push your limits. It also brings plenty of values that are useful every day, in every area of life.

Alongside that, I've been creating social media content since 2024: more than 3 million cumulative views, over 2,000 followers, and a partnership with a sports nutrition brand.

I also built this website myself, 100%. If you want a site like this one, I can teach you how to do it or build it for you.
