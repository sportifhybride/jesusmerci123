FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOT A QUESTION?

Is your coaching suited to all levels?

Yes. Whether you are a beginner or have trained for years, the plan is built on your real level, your schedule and your constraints.

Do you use an app for the training plans?

Your plan is in a shared Google Sheets: you check it on your phone or computer. It is updated every weekend.

How does the follow-up work?

You fill in your sessions and how you felt in the sheet. I review it every week and adjust the plan. A call every two weeks is possible, as an option.

Are the programs personalised?

The shop programs are structured frameworks. Coaching, on the other hand, is fully personalised after we talk.

How does payment work?

Payment is made online, via PayPal, Revolut or bank transfer. After paying, send me your receipt to receive your program.

Can you coach several sports at once?

Yes, and that is the idea behind hybrid coaching.

What minimum level do I need to start?

None. We build from your starting point.

Do I need any special equipment?

It depends on your goal. For running, a pair of shoes is enough to start.

How do I receive my program after payment?

After you pay, send me your receipt by email or on WhatsApp. Once I have checked your payment, I send you your program as a PDF.

What is the difference between a program and coaching?

A program is a structured PDF you follow alone, at your own pace. Coaching is fully personalised: we talk before starting, I build your plan, then I review your sessions and adjust the plan every week.

Are the programs open to minors?

Yes, the programs are open to minors. The purchase must be made with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

Can I get a refund?

No. Programs are digital content sent within 24 hours of your payment: before paying, you request this delivery and waive your right of withdrawal.
