BODYBUILDING

Two options for every program

WITHOUT FOLLOW-UP

You receive your program as a PDF and train on your own.

WITH FOLLOW-UP · +€5

You receive the PDF, a Google Sheet to track your sessions and regular contact with me on WhatsApp:

  • Recommended loads for your next sessions
  • Advice after each session
  • Loads adjusted to your fatigue
  • Feedback through the Sheet or by message
  • Tracking of your strength and progress

Duration: from 2 months for every program. The PDF is delivered within 24 hours of payment.

WHO IS IT FOR?

  • All levels: beginner, intermediate or advanced.
  • From age 14, with a parent's consent before 18. Not allowed under 14.
  • After 50, the PDFs remain available, but I strongly recommend tailored coaching: the body changes, and I adapt the approach to your age and condition.

WHAT YOU GET

  • A PDF program of 2 months minimum
  • Exercises explained, loads and percentages
  • With follow-up: a tracking table (Google Sheet) and WhatsApp contact
  • Without follow-up: no tracking table
