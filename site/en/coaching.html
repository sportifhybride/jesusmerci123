RUNNING COACHING

MY TRAINING PHILOSOPHY

Coaching built on a whole season, not a generic program.

01

A full season

The coaching can cover a whole sporting year. We build over time, with cycles that follow one another instead of starting from zero.

02

Winter: volume

In winter we build the aerobic base: more volume, easy paces, endurance work that prepares the rest of the season.

03

Summer: quality

In summer volume drops and quality rises: faster paces, speed and explosiveness work, to be a complete runner.

04

Your strengths first

I first work on what you already do well. A strength that is solidified becomes a weapon and pulls everything else up.

05

Volume matched to your level

Depending on your level and schedule, the number of weekly sessions varies from 3 to 6. Never fewer than 3, never more than 6.

06

Cross training

Cycling and strength work add volume without adding excessive fatigue or injury risk. Strength training, twice a week, at home or in the gym, is built in during the preparation period to prevent injuries, then removed during the taper. In winter it is mostly strengthening; in summer, more strength to perform on short, intense efforts.

07

A deload every 4 weeks

Every 4 weeks, a lighter week to absorb the work and avoid stagnation.

08

Two goals are possible

You can aim for several distances in the same season: for example a 10K and a half marathon, each with its own specific preparation.

09

I start by getting to know you

Your level, your past races, your goals, how many sessions per week you can run, and whether you play another sport alongside.

10

A plan that fits you

Your experience, level, time and daily constraints change the plan. Depending on your goal, the period, the preparation and the taper are not the same.

11

A complete runner

I work on your speed, your pace endurance and your base, on top of the specific work and your strengths. A unique method, based on science and experience.

12

Human support

Every day, I look at your sessions in your Google Sheet (or on Strava). You fill it in and I have access to it: you have nothing to send me. Every weekend, I rework your plan based on your feedback, and we talk about it by call or message.

What I want you to remember

Safety, progress, discipline and enjoyment. Enjoy it. When you think you are at your limit, you never are: the body is limited by the mind, and you can do far more than you think.

What I refuse to do: promise finishing times, or coach someone with no discipline or commitment.

REAL EXAMPLE

How it went for Fabio

A concrete example of how a season was organised, with his life constraints and schedule.

The week

Two running sessions in the general phase, three close to competition, two while tapering. Two to three strength sessions, plus a bike ride at the weekend when possible.

The volume

15 km a week at the start, up to a maximum of 30 km. Very gradual progression, without forcing.

The goals

Chained cycles, with a race roughly every two months. Enough to stay motivated without burning out.

The taper

Before a race: less strength work, more specific work, same volume but more quality, to be ready on race day.

Day-to-day follow-up

His plan lived in a shared Google Sheet: sessions and the athlete's feedback, updated every weekend. Available on phone and computer, and customisable at any time.

The contact

An optional call every two weeks to review progress and adjust if needed.

Every plan is built to measure. This example shows one possible organisation, not a model to copy exactly.
